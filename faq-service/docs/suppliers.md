# Suppliers and locations

## What is a supplier?

A supplier is a campus store, food outlet or facility that you can request items
from, such as a café, the NUS Co-op or a printer. Each supplier has a name, a
campus location, opening hours and, where available, a description and pickup
instructions.

## What kinds of suppliers are there?

Suppliers are grouped into three types: Food & Beverage, Retail, and
Facilities. You can filter by type on the "Suppliers" page.

## How do I find a supplier?

In Requester Mode, open "Suppliers". You can search by name or location and
filter by type. Each card shows the supplier's location, opening hours and
pickup notes.

## Why can't I create a request for a supplier?

You can only create a request for a supplier that is active and currently open.
If the supplier is closed or has been deactivated, FoC shows "Supplier is
inactive or currently closed." Check its opening hours and try again when it is
open.

## Where do I find a supplier's opening hours?

Opening hours and operating days are shown on each supplier's card on the
"Suppliers" page.

## How does the courier know where to pick up my items?

When you create a request, FoC saves the supplier's name and full pickup
location, including the building, floor, location description and any pickup
instructions, with the request. Couriers see this on the order. If the supplier
changes its details later, your existing request keeps the details from when it
was created.

## Can I request items from somewhere that isn't listed?

No. Requests must use one of FoC's listed suppliers. Admins add and update
suppliers. If a place you need is missing, ask an admin to add it.

## Who manages the supplier list?

Admins create, update and deactivate suppliers, including their photos, opening
hours and locations. Students can browse suppliers but cannot edit them.
