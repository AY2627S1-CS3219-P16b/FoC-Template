# Account and admin

## How do I update my profile?

Open "Profile". You can change your display name, contact preference, Telegram
handle, phone number and profile picture URL. Your email address cannot be
changed. Choose a preferred contact method and fill in that detail, so the
other person on an accepted errand can reach you; only that method is shared.

## What information is visible to other students?

Open listings identify requesters by a short student ID. After acceptance, the
requester and assigned courier can see each other's display name and preferred
contact only (Telegram, phone or email, if provided), through Delivered. Contact
is hidden once completion processing starts or the errand ends. Other students
cannot access these details through orders.

## What does my account status mean?

An active account can use FoC normally. Admins can suspend or disable an
account. Both have the same effect: you cannot log in or use FoC, and logging in
shows "This account has been suspended." or "This account has been disabled."
If it happens while you are logged in, you are returned to the login screen
within 15 minutes.

## I think my account was suspended by mistake. What should I do?

FoC does not yet have an in-app way to contact an admin or appeal a
suspension. Admins can reinstate an account by changing its status back to
active. An account review and reinstatement process is planned as future work.

## What can admins do?

Admins can do everything a regular student can, plus: view and update user
accounts, change account status, promote other students to admin or demote
them, view all orders, retry credit processing for orders marked as needing
attention, and manage the supplier list. Every admin change to a
user's profile, status or role is recorded in an audit log.

## How do I become an admin?

An existing admin must promote your account. The very first admin is set up
once by the team that runs FoC.

## Can I delete my account?

No. FoC does not support deleting accounts.
